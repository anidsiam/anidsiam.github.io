## Acceptance, Relief, and an Enduring Presence

---

I have prepared myself to see your happiness, sadness, relief, joy, pain, success, etc., as well as your life. The scene that hurt 99% of peoples, could not hurt me that day, rather it gave me relief. The personality that needed to be possessed to bring myself to this 1% was naturally inherent in me. which is a matter of great luck. Thanks God.

You are a simple human and I have philosophical disagreement with you. But I couldn't stop thinking about your existence. Just because, I were involved buddy, nothing more than that.

---

*October 20, 2024*
