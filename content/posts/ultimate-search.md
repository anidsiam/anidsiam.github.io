# Ultimate Search

---

With sleepy eyes, in hushed stillness,  
I grasp a hundred elusive things,  
And paint a thousand images!

Holding onto countless emotions,  
I heave a myriad of sighs;  
Bearing the shackles of destiny,  
I fearlessly seek a moment's joy!

Enduring a hundred unbearable trials,  
Amidst the neglect of weary faith,  
I exhale a poisonous breath;

Yet, beyond all the greyness,  
I weave dreams of vibrant color—  
Let this earthly life remain grey;  
It is a colorful hereafter  
That I ultimately seek!

---

*March 23, 2022*
